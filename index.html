How this first version works

  1. Use Planner to save tasks on this device.
  2. Open AI helper and paste in a question.
  3. Choose the type of help you need and create a prompt.
  4. Copy it or open ChatGPT, then paste it into the chat.
  5. Ask follow-up questions until you understand the method.
This starter app does not secretly access your school account, submit homework, or call an AI service in the background. The AI step happens in ChatGPT after you choose to open it.

Privacy

Your planner items are stored locally in this browser on this device. Avoid entering private information about yourself or other people. Clearing browser data may erase your saved tasks.
